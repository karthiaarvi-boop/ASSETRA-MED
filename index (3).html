Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some answers to help you understand Assetra Med.

1. What is Assetra Med?

Assetra Med is a web platform based in Coimbatore that connects small hospitals, biomedical service engineers, and medical equipment companies.

2. Which equipment does Assetra Med support?

We are starting with multi-parameter patient monitors and will expand to other equipment over time.

3. Can hospitals find biomedical engineers?

Yes. Hospitals can search for nearby biomedical service engineers and check their ratings.

4. How does QR-based equipment tracking work?

You register a piece of equipment and generate its QR code. Scanning the code gives quick access to its details and service history.

5. Can biomedical engineers register?

Yes. Biomedical engineers can register to connect with hospitals that need their services.

6. Is Assetra Med free?

Early access is currently free. Some services, such as the equipment check, are subject to confirmation.

7. Is Assetra Med currently available?

We are in our early development stage and are opening early access. Register your interest to be notified.

8. Where is Assetra Med based?

Assetra Med is based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.
